Dash-OS Privacy Policy

Team Powers Smart Operations System

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Dash-OS is an internal operations portal for Team Powers employees and authorized business users. This policy explains how information is handled when an authorized user accesses the portal.

Information we collect

When you sign in, we receive basic account information from the authentication provider, such as your name, email address, and profile image. Dash-OS may also store information you submit while using its scheduling, KPI, ordering, support, receipt, and related workplace tools.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate users, apply role- and store-based access, operate Team Powers workflows, keep business records, improve the portal, and protect the security and reliability of the service.

Sharing

Information is used for Team Powers business operations and is available only to authorized personnel according to their responsibilities. We do not sell personal information. Service providers may process limited information when necessary to host, secure, authenticate, or operate Dash-OS.

Data retention and security

Business records are retained for as long as reasonably needed for operational, legal, accounting, and security purposes. We use access controls and reputable hosting and authentication providers to reduce unauthorized access, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may review or change the information available through your connected account. To request correction or deletion of Dash-OS account information, contact Team Powers. Some business records may need to be retained when required for legitimate operational or legal purposes.

Contact

Questions about this policy or Dash-OS data practices may be sent to daustin@powerspizza.com.
